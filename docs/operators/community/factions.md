**Where:** Open **More → Factions**.

The Factions tab is currently an overview rather than a complete management surface. It lists player-created groups with their name, tag, level, experience, and member count.

Use the search field to find a faction by name or tag, then select it for the detail view available in the current portal version.

Faction management is under active development, and the portal warns that its features and data may change. Avoid writing staff procedures that depend on an unfinished action until it is exposed in the portal and covered here.

Viewing factions requires `factions.view`.
