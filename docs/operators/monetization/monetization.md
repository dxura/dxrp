**Where:** Open **More → Monetization**.

Monetization contains four operator workflows: products, coupons, sales, and earnings breakdowns. Addon authors set addon pricing from [Addons](/docs/operators/addons/addons), while this page manages products sold by your network.

## Products

A product can grant:

- a rank;
- an inventory item; or
- a manual action your staff fulfils outside the automatic grant system.

Choose one-time or monthly billing, set the price and purchase limit, add a description and image, and configure the post-purchase return URL when needed.

Use **Copy Link** to get the DXRP checkout URL. Do not construct checkout links yourself.

## Manual grants

Use **Grant** when a player needs a complimentary or corrected entitlement. Confirm the player and product first; a manual grant should not be used to bypass normal purchase records.

## Coupons

Coupons can apply a percentage or fixed-dollar discount. Set an optional expiry, total redemption cap, and per-player cap. Disable or delete a code when a campaign ends.

## Sales and breakdown

The sales table is the transaction-level record. The breakdown summarises earnings and platform fees. Configure the payment recipient under [Network](/docs/operators/administration/network) before relying on payouts.

Access is permission-based across monetization, products, and coupons, so staff may see only some tabs.
